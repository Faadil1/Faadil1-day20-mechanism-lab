# Multi-LLM Pre-Concept Synthesis

GPT is the central synthesizer. External reviews were used as upstream research inputs, not as authorities or votes.

## Gemini
Path as cognition: friction, spatial memory, orientation, provenance. Useful but broad memory-degradation claims were rejected.

## Grok
Path as infrastructure: durable edges, handoff, exit, link decay. The claim that personal old-web journeys were normally public objects was rejected.

## Claude
Path as provenance: who or what produced the next transition? Added answer-collapse and causal retraceability.

## DeepSeek — bonus review
Before/behind the path: vague intent, folk theories, and the double meaning of interaction as expression plus training signal.

## Perplexity
Path as state change: the system observes the traveler and changes the terrain, creating a feedback loop whose consequences can persist.

## Kimi
Revealed behavior versus endorsed preference. Added the strongest mechanism candidate: retrospective mismatch between what generated engagement and what the user says they wanted the session to achieve.

## Convergence

The original nostalgia hypothesis did not survive intact.

Current model:

WHAT I WANT
→ WHAT I DO
→ WHAT THE SYSTEM THINKS I WANT
→ WHAT THE SYSTEM CHANGES
→ WHAT I ENCOUNTER NEXT
→ WHAT I DO NEXT
↺

The key uncertainty is not whether algorithms are good or bad. It is whether users can perceive, reconstruct, or endorse the causal conversion from behavior into model state and then into environment.
