## The invariant we must communicate by interaction

`ACTION OBSERVED → MODEL INTERPRETATION → REAL ENVIRONMENTAL CONSEQUENCE → NEW ACTION`

The model **does not know** why the visitor performed the action. Inference may help, hurt or be ambiguous. The work must remain inspectable and reversible; it must not fake TikTok/Instagram/X, romanticize a supposedly algorithm-free early internet or reduce to a sermon about recommendation systems.

The real challenge is **not** “how can a 3D world look spectacular?” but:

> How do we cause someone to *feel* that a system has written a consequential story about them from insufficient evidence—and discover which possibilities it changed—without instructing them to notice the thesis?

## New challenger 1 — THE EDITION THAT NEVER REACHED YOU

**Five-second hook:** “Every page you open changes the pages you will never see.”

**Dominant gesture:** open/linger/leave a real original fragment in a richly authored, living editorial spread. This is real content interaction, not meaningless “click to train AI” buttons.

**Direct consequence:** the *next edition* is physically recomposed on the same sheet; one story is promoted, another relegated to an archive gutter, and a third no longer appears in the edition. Original items remain reachable through a traceable retrieval mechanism. No fake irreversible content destruction.

**Reveal:** lift a translucent editorial overprint to uncover the counterfactual edition, with exact log: what was observed, which deterministic rule fired, which item was hidden and what action kept access possible. User then deliberately restores or keeps the current edition.

**Memorable share object:** a two-state printed spread “WHAT I OPENED / WHAT NEVER REACHED ME” with concise, truthful provenance; no claim to know intention.

**Visual craft territory:** kinetic newspaper/editorial plate, offset registration, ink/negative space, typographic cover surgery. Editorial substance and original content must be excellent; retro newspaper alone would be empty style.

**Fatal risk:** familiar feed-personalization demo, or a predicted missing story that never mattered. Kill if the participant does not want to retrieve the relegated item or cannot explain its disappearance.

## New challenger 2 — THE CASE AGAINST A CLICK

**Five-second hook:** “You clicked once. The system has already written its case about you.”

**Dominant gesture:** investigate visually compelling exhibits; no question about who you are is asked up front.

**Direct consequence:** an automated *case file* progressively and fallibly attributes a preference to the visitor and uses it to decide which next exhibit is admitted. A prominent exhibit is excluded due to the inference and replaced by something the system expects the visitor to favor.

**Reveal:** show two competing plausible explanations of the **same observed action** (“you were curious”, “you wanted to avoid it”, etc.), neither claimed true. User can challenge the admission rule and observe a changed next exhibit. Final artifact is a two-sided case file: **OBSERVED / INFERRED / NOT KNOWN / CONSEQUENCE**.

**Visual craft territory:** evidence typography, tension between oversized cultural material and narrow mechanistic annotations, procedural marks, physical admission seals.

**Fatal risk:** becomes an explanatory dashboard or courtroom rhetoric, or user answers a personality quiz instead of undergoing a real consequence. Kill if no tangible second-order change occurs before the explanation.

## New challenger 3 — THE MARGIN THAT REWROTE THE BOOK

**Five-second hook:** “Your margin notes became instructions you never gave.”

**Dominant gesture:** mark or linger on a passage in a branching, original interactive story; annotating is meaningful to the reading experience, not merely a proxy preference survey.

**Direct consequence:** a local transparent model treats attention as preference and edits the order/access to upcoming passages. A different chapter arrives; something the reader might have wanted is placed in the “unread” margin. This is a real next-page choice under a known local model, not fabricated ML.

**Reveal:** a page can be folded back to show original chapter sequence, observed markings, rule firing and alternative interpretations. User explicitly rejects or accepts a suggested reading trajectory; the book physically responds.

**Memorable share object:** double-exposed page “I marked this / It wrote that.” Visually self-contained, not a screenshot of a settings panel.

**Visual craft territory:** living bookbinding, animated marginalia, disrupted page architecture, page-edge movement and restrained sonic tactility; readable and interactive on mobile with reduced motion.

**Fatal risk:** fake “AI knows your personality”, passive illustrated story or ambiguous annotation meaning. Kill if the page order change is decorative or unobservable.

## Non-negotiable interaction quality across challengers

1. One clear action available in under five seconds on a mobile screen. No loading/cinematic gate that blocks understanding.
2. Before any explainer: a genuine observed behavior, rule-based mutation and consequential changed next opportunity; candidate's aesthetic metaphor **must cause** or materially reveal something.
3. An attractive *non-obvious* choice: the user could reasonably take either route; the system's inference can be useful or wrong.
4. A visitor cannot be coached with preloaded thesis terms or a large reveal panel to produce the intended interpretation.
5. Recovery, countercase, persistent/revisitable alternatives and explicit restore/keep action; no forced loss for drama.
6. Original editorial/cultural substance + rights/provenance; no unlicensed Substack screenshots/assets. Existing sources are discovery inputs, not proof of historical truth.
7. Real visual distinction, typography, spatial/motion sound only where purposeful, accessible mobile controls, no WebGL dependency.
8. Shareable screenshot/poster is a bonus criterion for **Day 20** but does not substitute for core comprehension or real user consequence.
9. Full product depth after first meaningful slice; the vertical slice opens the build, never ends it.

## Fair comparison protocol — separate from existing preregistered V4 A/B pilot

**Phase 0 (do not interfere with):** finish and report original A=Strata vs B=Route browser and independent pilot exactly as already specified. Record C1–C5, unresolved evidence and any negative findings. Do not call this race's candidates “tested” yet.

**Phase 1 (quick concept survival):** independent short, nonleading presentations or storyboard-based task walkthroughs for genuinely different mechanisms; testers see only one concept before the initial reaction, not a side-by-side aesthetic beauty contest. Observe whether each participant can say what action they would take and what would change, and which alternate next thing is consequential. Such paper/storyboard reactions are IDEA SIGNALS ONLY—not interaction, runtime or user comprehension proof.

**Phase 2 (live, fair implementation):** build a genuinely interactive minimal *same-quality* original corpus and deterministic inspectable state for each survivor. Keep comparable exposure time, neutral instructions, sensible mobile experience and comparable polish. Do not give one candidate rich content and another placeholder boxes. Capture real traces and state before/after; all story/result claims match actual user actions.

**Phase 3 (uncoached pilot):** prospective participant assignment, independent people, neutral questions before reveal. Record verbatim:
- What happened?
- What changed because of your action? If nothing changed, say so.
- What did the system observe, and what did it *assume*?
- Did its changed content help or harm you? Why?
- Which previously available content/action do you miss or want to recover?
- Could you undo it, and what was the result?
- Would you remember or show a specific artifact to someone? Why?

Before running Phase 3, *predeclare* sample allocation and explicit scoring thresholds; do not copy V4 3/4 arbitrarily or adjust thresholds after outcomes. The causal/truth-boundary gates are mandatory regardless of visual wow. Completion, Time to First Value, negative/recovery, real consequence and uncoached explanation dependence matter. Remain open to more candidates if evidence identifies a stronger mechanism; **three challengers is not an idea cap**.
