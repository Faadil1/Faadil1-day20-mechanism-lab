## Visual/product observations

The conditions are **research prototypes**, not the proposed cinematic Sediment Atlas final world.

Strength:
- clear map node affordances;
- readable geological color hierarchy;
- route buttons usable without interacting with SVG itself;
- original study specimens provide a reason to branch from the archive;
- visible revision marker ties decisions to model changes.

Gap:
- layers look like a largely decorative backdrop behind an overlaid path diagram;
- revision 0 → 1 is not yet an obvious, spatially dramatic geological event in the observed frames;
- navigation primarily happens by selecting passage buttons, not bodily traveling through a geological environment;
- a wide desktop layout leaves large visual/interaction separation between stage map and controls;
- the archive content is intentionally a thin original fictional corpus.

These are product-design learning points, not evidence that the V4 hypothesis has failed. Do not convert a visual or aesthetic impression into a comprehension claim.
