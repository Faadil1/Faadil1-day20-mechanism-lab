## Product / research problem

Modern recommendation environments may use observable behavior as evidence about what a person wants, act on that model, and alter what the person encounters next even when the person's actual intention is unknown.

The project is testing whether that conversion can be made **felt and causally legible** through interaction rather than explained through an essay, nostalgia piece, fake feed or transparency dashboard.

Working law:

> **Behavior is evidence. It is not intention.**

Working research question:

> **What happens when a system learns what you do faster than you can tell it what you mean?**

## Intended participant

A general web user who understands ordinary clicking, saving, skipping, lingering and navigation but is not briefed on the thesis before using the experiment.

No claim is made that this participant represents all social-media or recommender-system users.

## Job to be done

Let a participant experience a small system in which:
1. they act naturally;
2. the system uses those actions as bounded signals;
3. the environment or ranking changes;
4. the participant later sees what was observed versus what was only inferred;
5. the experiment does not pretend to know their true intention.

## Goals

- make `behavior → inference → consequence` observable through real product behavior;
- preserve `UNKNOWN` when intention cannot be established;
- discover which mechanism communicates causal ambiguity without explanatory coaching;
- keep the system deterministic and inspectable;
- generate evidence from real participant use before Concept Lock;
- preserve counter-cases rather than moralize against recommendation systems.

## Non-goals

- recreate the 1990s/2000s web;
- claim that algorithms are inherently harmful;
- claim that chronological feeds are superior;
- prove filter bubbles, radicalization or universal memory harm;
- simulate proprietary TikTok, Instagram, X, YouTube or Meta ranking logic;
- infer mental health, identity or sensitive traits;
- build an algorithm-control dashboard;
- lock a public brand before the mechanism survives.

## Current candidate mechanisms

### A — Retrospective Mismatch
Participant states a session goal. A deterministic local model updates from observable actions. The reveal compares stated session intent with resulting model state without treating either as ground truth.

### B — Who Moved the World?
A visible information terrain changes as observed visits update a deterministic similarity model. The terrain moves; participant intention remains unknown.

### C — Unretraceable Cause
A sequence is dynamically ranked from prior observable actions. The participant later retrieves an item and tries to reconstruct why it appeared at that point.

## Current architecture

- one static `index.html`;
- HTML/CSS/vanilla JavaScript;
- local deterministic model rules;
- localStorage evidence log;
- no backend;
- no external model;
- no proprietary algorithm simulation;
- Cloudflare Pages preview for blind testing.

## Truth invariants

- observed action may be logged as **OBSERVED**;
- model update generated by local rule may be logged as **OBSERVED**;
- preference derived from action is **INFERRED**;
- true intention is **UNKNOWN** unless explicitly stated;
- a live preview is not production evidence;
- a local deterministic proof is not external-user evidence;
- missing runtime↔commit binding remains visible.

## Product Reality

AI advantage: **NOT_APPLICABLE_WITH_REASON**. The current core value does not require an AI model; adding one would weaken causal inspectability without demonstrated user value.

Primary outcome for this phase:
**Can a blind participant independently describe the behavior → inference → consequence relationship while recognizing that intention was not actually known?**

## Negative event / counter-case

Source-verifiable negative event carried into the project:
observable or revealed preferences can drive engagement/enjoyment while diverging from reflectively endorsed or ideal preferences.

Required counter-case:
algorithmic mediation can also improve relevance, discovery, support and convenience. The experiment must not treat human or chronological curation as inherently superior.

## Current user flow

1. participant opens one blind probe URL;
2. no thesis is disclosed;
3. participant interacts;
4. local deterministic state changes;
5. participant sees a bounded session trace;
6. interviewer runs `BLIND-TEST-SCRIPT.md`;
7. result is recorded without coaching;
8. evidence across probes is compared before Concept Lock.

## Acceptance criteria for Concept Lock

Concept Lock remains blocked until:
- multiple blind participants have used the mechanisms;
- at least one mechanism repeatedly produces causal comprehension without thesis coaching;
- participants distinguish observed behavior from inferred intention;
- the winning mechanism survives a renewed competitive/collision check;
- no material truth-boundary violation remains;
- the current evidence graph accurately reflects runtime and deployment gaps.

## Product Depth & Live Reality — current scope

The Cloudflare preview is a real user-facing surface and the probes perform real local state changes. This is a **live research vertical slice**, not a completed product.

Post-vertical-slice Product Exploitation is therefore active, but bounded to:
- real external-user testing;
- stronger causal consequence where justified by results;
- failure/boundary/recovery depth;
- observability/receipt quality;
- runtime/commit binding;
- self-serve clarity;
- only then, additional product depth justified by the mechanism that survives.

No arbitrary feature expansion is authorized before the mechanism proves useful.

## Key risks

- participants understand only “algorithms are bad”;
- B's movement is too subtle to notice;
- C feels like a memory puzzle instead of causal uncertainty;
- A's stated-vs-model comparison feels like a dashboard;
- project claims a universal human effect from narrow research;
- visual polish primes interpretation and invalidates the blind test;
- Cloudflare deployment cannot be bound to an exact Git commit;
- product scope grows before mechanism validity is established.
